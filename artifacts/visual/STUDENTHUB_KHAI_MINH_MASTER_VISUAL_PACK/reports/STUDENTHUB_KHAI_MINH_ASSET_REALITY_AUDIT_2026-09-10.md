## 1. Executive Summary & Reality Policy
The Khai Minh visual assets provide extraordinary aesthetic depth, cinematic lighting, and symbolic coherence for StudentHub AI. However, several assets contain **simulated UI, placeholder metrics, fictional expert names, synthetic citations, and concept dashboards**. 

To prevent user deception, false advertising, or hallucinated product claims:
1. **Human Beings**: All human subjects depicted (students, researchers, hands annotating paper) are strictly **ILLUSTRATIVE_ONLY**. Never classify or present them as real students, verified mentors, or real credential reviewers.
2. **Statistics & Claims**: Numbers like *"500K+ Học viên"*, *"1,200+ Chuyên gia"*, *"10,000+ Tài liệu"*, *"50+ Quốc gia"* are visual concept ornaments. They must **NEVER** appear as factual platform metrics.
3. **Citations & Cases**: Case titles like *"The Story of a Lake (2024)"* or citations like *"Nguyen et al. 2023"* in artwork are narrative props. Live product evidence must be backed by real verified database records.
4. **Interactive States**: No embedded bitmap UI (buttons, search inputs, toggles) may be treated as live UI. All operational controls must be rendered in the DOM.
