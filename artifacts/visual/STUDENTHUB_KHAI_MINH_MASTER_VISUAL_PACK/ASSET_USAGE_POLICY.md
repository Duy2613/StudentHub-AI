# 🛡️ StudentHub AI — Khai Minh Asset Usage Policy
> **Document**: `ASSET_USAGE_POLICY.md` | **Authority**: Phase A Visual Governance

## 1. Non-Negotiable Rules
1. **Generated Humans = Illustrative Only**: No person in these images may ever be represented as a real student, verified expert, or platform reviewer.
2. **Embedded Text = Decorative Concept Only**: Text drawn inside bitmaps cannot serve as live interface text or factual copy. Real copy must be rendered via semantic HTML/JSX.
3. **Simulated UI != Live State**: Concept mockups (Dashboard, Settings, Cases) are visual inspiration only, not operational application states.
4. **Synthetic Citations != Trust Evidence**: Papers and citations depicted in imagery cannot be presented as real verified scholarly sources.
5. **Simulated Metrics != Product Claims**: Figures such as "500K+ students", "1,200+ experts", "93% confidence" must never be stated as factual marketing claims.
6. **Immutable Originals**: Source intake assets must never be overwritten, modified in-place, or directly deployed to `/public`.
7. **Responsive Optimization Required**: Only processed WebP/AVIF derivatives respecting performance budgets may be served in production.
