# Community ↔ Expert Bridge

Community can detect high-value disagreement and enqueue a sanitized Expert
request containing a domain/topic, revision, claim, cited signal IDs, and a
reason. The queue has no assignment, qualification, SLA, or authority effect.

Only existing Expert services may select a verified Expert, enforce conflict
of interest, assign a case revision, submit an assessment, or change Expert
authority. Community displays the result only through existing canonical Expert
read models. Contributor quality and helpfulness remain qualification signals
and never grant Expert authority.
