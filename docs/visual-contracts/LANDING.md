# Visual Handoff — Landing

**Status:** `READY_FOR_ANTIGRAVITY`  
**Feature:** `/` product landing  
**Owner:** Antigravity visual; Luna product/contract authority  
**Date:** 2026-09-01

## USER GOAL

Understand that StudentHub AI helps students investigate before believing, then enter Trust quickly and discover Community and Expert support without feature sprawl.

## INFO HIERARCHY

1. Trust-first promise and primary entry.
2. How investigation becomes evidence-aware action.
3. Community corroboration and Expert escalation as supporting pillars.
4. Case Lab/personal workspace context.

## DATA CONTRACT

Landing may use static product copy and existing approved assets. Any metric, provider result, expert authority, community count, or Trust verdict must come from an approved contract; absent data stays absent. Primary destination is `/trust`; supporting destinations are `/community`, `/expert`, and `/cases`.

## STATE CONTRACT

Landing interactions are `IDLE`, `LOADING` only where an actual asset/route operation exists, and explicit `ERROR`/`UNAVAILABLE` where applicable. Do not show fabricated live metrics or simulated Trust findings.

## INTERACTION

Primary CTA opens Trust. Secondary CTAs explain or open Community/Expert/Case Lab. Preserve keyboard navigation, reduced-motion fallback, direct deep links, and safe external-link behavior.

## DESKTOP / TABLET / MOBILE

- Desktop: Trust should dominate the first decision point and navigation.
- Tablet: retain the same CTA priority with legible supporting pillars.
- Mobile: present a short, fast Trust entry; do not bury the primary action beneath cinematic media.

## ACCESSIBILITY

Use one clear page heading, meaningful link names, alt text, focus states, reduced-motion support, and no autoplay requirement for understanding the product.

## PERFORMANCE BUDGET

Keep landing critical path free of heavy 3D/video/model work. Measure any new media before adoption and preserve the existing no-overflow/mobile and build gates.

## ALLOWED FILES

Landing composition, existing approved assets, type/spacing/color application, restrained motion, and landing-focused tests.

## FORBIDDEN FILES

New product pillars/routes, fake metrics, backend/API/auth changes, Trust contract changes, unapproved media/3D/WebGL, or visual claims that require missing data.

## VISUAL FREEDOM

Antigravity may shape the editorial/cinematic landing language, hero composition, section rhythm, and motion after the semantic CTA/data rules are preserved.

## SEMANTIC RESTRICTIONS

No landing flourish may obscure Trust, imply product capability that is not implemented, or turn static/demo content into live evidence.

## ACCEPTANCE

Trust-first CTA is obvious at all breakpoints; Community/Expert remain supporting; no fake metrics; route/accessibility/performance gates pass; advanced cinematic work is separately reviewed by Antigravity and the human owner.
