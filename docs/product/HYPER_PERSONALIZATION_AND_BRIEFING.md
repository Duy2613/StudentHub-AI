## 1. Information Prioritization Hierarchy

```text
1. Explicit User Request
       ↓
2. Explicit Academic Goals (UserGoalEngine)
       ↓
3. Current Academic Context (Enrolled courses, active semester)
       ↓
4. Recent Relevant Activity
       ↓
5. General Preferences
       ↓
6. Weak Inferences (lowest weight)
```

---

## 2. "My Academic Briefing" Composition

The daily briefing dynamically synthesizes 6 critical intelligence dimensions:
1. **Important Changes**: Promulgated circulars, room adjustments, syllabus updates.
2. **Upcoming Deadlines**: Tuition deadlines, course registration dates, exam schedules.
3. **Matched Verified Expert**: Top-ranked professor or advisor with institutional verification.
4. **Community Signals**: Active operational reports from student peers.
5. **Unresolved Contradiction**: Highlights operational divergence between statutory policy and real-world execution.
6. **Grounded Recommended Actions**: Next best actions with transparent *"Why am I seeing this?"* and supporting evidence.
