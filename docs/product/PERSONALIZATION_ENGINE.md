## 1. Persona Taxonomy

```text
• NEW_STUDENT: 0 - 30 Earned Credits (Focus on foundation prerequisites & orientation)
• STUDENT: 31 - 99 Earned Credits (Focus on core roadmap & elective planning)
• SENIOR_STUDENT: 100+ Earned Credits (Focus on graduation requirements & capstone)
• EXPERT: Verified Academic Advisor / Faculty Member
• LECTURER: Instructor in charge of syllabus & grading
• MODERATOR: Community accuracy & health auditor
• STAFF_ADMIN: University administration & policy coordinator
```

## 2. Explainable Next Best Action ("Why am I seeing this?")

Every card generated in the Personal Command Center is paired with:
1. **Explainable Rationale**: Transparent justification based on enrolled courses and cohort.
2. **Supporting Evidence**: Direct pointer to promulgated regulation or syllabus handbook.
3. **Alternative Pathways**: Non-locking fallback suggestions.
