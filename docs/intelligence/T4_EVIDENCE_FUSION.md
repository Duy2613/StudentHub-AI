## 2. The 6-Type Contradiction Engine

The `ContradictionEngine` classifies conflicts into:

| Conflict Type | Description | Resolution Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| `DIRECT_CONTRADICTION` | Mutually exclusive propositions in same scope | Prioritize higher statutory authority; flag if peer level |
| `TEMPORAL_CONFLICT` | Revision change between 2025 and 2026 regulations | Update to latest active promulgated version |
| `SCOPE_CONFLICT` | Divergence between Mass and High-Quality programs | Maintain separate cohort branches |
| `PARTIAL_CONTRADICTION` | Discrepancy in prerequisite list or credit count | Fix to canonical curriculum handbook |
| `SOURCE_VERSION_CONFLICT`| Draft notice vs Rector promulgation | Rector promulgation wins |
| `APPARENT_CONFLICT` | Surface-level wording differences | Reconcile semantically |

---

## 3. Conflict Resolution with Preserved Nuance

When an official statutory rule (e.g. "Registration closes Friday 24:00") conflicts with community experience (e.g. "Portal crashes at 22:00"):
- **Primary Verdict**: `SUPPORTED` (Statutory rule holds).
- **Preserved Operational Nuance**: Community instability warning attached to recommendation.
