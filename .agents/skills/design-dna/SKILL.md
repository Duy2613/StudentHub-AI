# Design DNA — Anti-Template Aesthetic & Typographic Engineering

## 1. Core Philosophy
Prevent "AI Slop" and generic SaaS templates by enforcing calibrated design DNA:
- **Optical Typography Ratio**: Strict visual contrast between Editorial Serif (emotion, storytelling, identity) and Modern Clean Sans (interface, metadata, actions).
- **Asymmetric Editorial Rhythm**: Dynamic spatial composition with generous whitespace, structured negative space, and academic reference annotations.
- **Physics-Based Multi-Layer Shadows**: Layered ambient occlusion + directional specular reflection instead of muddy generic drop-shadows.

## 2. Typography Rules & Tracking Matrix
1. **Hero Headlines (Display Serif)**:
   - Line 1: Expressive Editorial Serif (`font-serif`, `italic`, `tracking-tight`, `leading-[1.05]`).
   - Line 2: Heavy Geometric Sans (`font-sans`, `font-black`, `tracking-tighter`, `leading-[1.0]`).
2. **Metadata & Section Headers (Academic Annotation)**:
   - Uppercase mono/sans metadata: `font-mono`, `text-[10px]` to `text-xs`, `tracking-[0.2em]`, `font-bold`.
   - Prefix with chapter indexing: `01 / IDENTIFY`, `02 / VERIFY`, `03 / COLLABORATE`.
3. **Body Text**:
   - `font-sans`, `text-sm` or `text-base`, `text-gray-300`, `leading-relaxed` ($1.6$ to $1.75$ line height), `max-w-prose` to prevent long unreadable lines.

## 3. Spatial Alignment & Grid Physics
- **Golden Ratio & Column Alignment**: Avoid centered-everything layout. Use structured asymmetric offsets (e.g. Left $60\%$ editorial text & narrative, Right $40\%$ interactive live viewport/hologram).
- **Card Bezel Chemistry**: Concentric double-bezel radii ($R_{outer} = 32\text{px}$, $R_{inner} = R_{outer} - \text{padding}$).
- **Subtle Surface Tactility**: Backdrop blur ($16\text{px} - 32\text{px}$), ultra-fine borders (`border-white/10`), ambient light gradient sweeps.
